{% comment %} Whole-game analysis. Nothing queues analysis on a schedule — an imported archive is far more games than a worker could ever get through — so this is where it starts. The POST is idempotent (`enqueue_game_analysis` skips what is already queued or done), so a second press costs nothing. Once every move is analysed the button turns into a re-run: the same endpoint with `force=1`, which queues every move again and overwrites the analyses on record. That is the one thing the idempotent POST cannot do, and without it a finished game has nothing left to offer. While the queue drains this block polls itself. It has to: the coach card is what normally refreshes the page's fragments, and it does not poll at all unless the *selected* ply is in flight — press "Analyse this game" from the starting position and nothing would ever move. `hx-select` picks this block back out of the full position view, and `hx-select-oob` takes with it the three fragments a completed analysis also changes: the history gets its new entry, the grid its badge, the bar its eval. Without them the counter would climb while the timeline it is counting stayed empty. Everything else — board, coach card, nav — is left alone, so the card's own 2s poll is not disturbed and nothing the user is looking at is replaced under them.
